1. Who We Are

Welcome to Swing Monkey Arcade (swing-monkey.github.io). Founded by veteran web developers and physics-based puzzle aficionados, our platform began with a simple passion: recreating the fluid, momentum-driven thrill of rope-swinging and pendulum dynamics directly in modern web browsers without plugins, installations, or paywalls.

What started as an interactive showcase for our flagship physics platformer Swing Monkey quickly evolved into a curated repository of over 140+ premium browser titles spanning high-speed racing, tactical shooting, brain-bending puzzles, and multiplayer sports challenges.

Why Browser Gaming Matters: Traditional gaming platforms often burden players with multi-gigabyte downloads, hardware restrictions, and intrusive updates. We believe high-quality gaming should be instant, cross-platform, accessible, and lightweight.

2. Our Core Mission

Our core mission revolves around three non-negotiable pillars: Instant Playability, Uncompromising Safety, and Honest Transparency.

Zero-Lag Performance

Every title is optimized to load under 2 seconds, maintaining smooth 60 FPS across both low-spec school Chromebooks and high-end rigs.

Safe & Family Friendly

We enforce strict content filtering: zero malware, zero deceptive redirects, and zero harmful pop-ups, adhering strictly to COPPA guidelines.

100% Free Access

No subscriptions, no hidden micro-transactions, and no locked levels. We keep browser gaming genuinely free and democratic for all.

3. Our E-E-A-T Quality Commitment

Google's Search Quality Rater Guidelines emphasize Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, and Trustworthiness (E-E-A-T). Here is how Swing Monkey Arcade upholds these standards in every piece of content and game we deliver:

Experience (First-Hand Play)

We do not auto-crawl or bulk-import games. Our testing curators physically complete multiple levels of every single game to evaluate mechanics, hitbox precision, control responsiveness, and difficulty curves before publication.

Expertise (Technical Insight)

Our editorial team includes HTML5 Canvas, WebGL, and WebAssembly engineers who verify frame timing, memory leakage, audio sync, and cross-browser rendering accuracy across Chrome, Edge, Safari, and Firefox.

Authoritativeness (Reputable Curation)

We partner directly with original game studios and reputable distribution partners. Each title includes official attribution, verified release metadata, and canonical guides created by our staff.

Trustworthiness (Security & Ethics)

We operate with complete legal transparency: active DMCA takedown procedures, clear Google AdSense disclosures, strict GDPR/CCPA privacy compliance, and dedicated direct human support.

4. Our 5-Stage QA & Device Testing Pipeline

Before any game appears on Swing Monkey Arcade, it must successfully pass through our rigorous 5-stage quality evaluation checklist:

  1. Security & Sandboxing Audit: Each game's code is audited for external network calls, intrusive cryptocurrency mining scripts, and unexpected trackers. Games must run safely in secure, sandboxed iframes.
  2. Chromebook & Hardware Benchmarking: We test game performance on typical school-issued Chromebooks (Intel Celeron / 4GB RAM) as well as mobile touchscreens to ensure consistent 50–60 FPS playback.
  3. Input & Latency Calibration: We verify keyboard (WASD / Arrows), mouse, and multi-touch inputs to confirm zero input lag and eliminate key-ghosting problems.
  4. Family Appropriateness & COPPA Review: Content is screened for excessive gore, inappropriate language, or aggressive monetization. Only titles suitable for broad audiences are approved.
  5. Audio & Usability Sanity Check: Verification of audio controls, mute buttons, fullscreen capabilities, and theater mode toggle states for accessibility.
Rejection Rate: Over 45% of submitted games are rejected or requested for optimization revisions during our QA process. We prioritize user experience over sheer volume.

6. Technology & Security Architecture

We deploy our infrastructure on world-class, globally distributed edge content delivery networks (CDNs). Key architectural aspects include:

  • Strict HTTPS: All game assets and scripts are served over encrypted TLS 1.3 channels.
  • No Obsolete Flash Dependencies: 100% of our catalog runs on modern HTML5, Canvas, WebGL, or WebAssembly APIs.
  • Isolated Sandboxing: External game iframes are restricted using modern browser sandbox permissions (e.g., allow-scripts allow-same-origin) to prevent unauthorized redirects or intrusive cookie storage.
  • Zero Local Data Harvesting: We do not ask for player registrations, passwords, or personal credentials to play any game.
