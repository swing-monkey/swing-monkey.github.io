About Swing Monkey

Swing Monkey is a fun physics-based arcade game where you help a playful monkey navigate through lush jungle courses. By swinging from peg to peg, building momentum, and launching through the air, your goal is to reach the finish line safely!

Tips to Score High

Watch your release angle! Releasing the vine when the monkey is at a 45-degree upward trajectory gives you maximum height and distance. Be sure to land on trampolines along the way to skip long gaps!
